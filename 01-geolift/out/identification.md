SAMPLE: What GeoLift identifies (15 lines)
GeoLift identifies an ATT: incremental cash sales in treated geos versus a synthetic control of untreated geos, for a tactic you actually geo-targeted on or off.
The package column is Y; in operator language that KPI is cash sales, not platform-reported conversions or ROAS.
The design needs a clean holdout: control markets must not receive the treatment during the window.
It does not identify national TV. You cannot hold a DMA out of a national linear buy without breaking the buy.
It does not identify always-on brand you will not pause. A holdout that never ships is not an experiment.
It does not identify spillover. Commuting, IP leakage, and national social that still serves control bias ATT toward zero.
It does not identify a child cell from a parent test. Meta-all-up does not pin ASC versus prospecting.
Power is not identification. A four-period test can be valid and still miss a 5% MDE at 80% power.
MTA is not incrementality. Last-click and multi-touch assign credit among converters; they never hold out spend.
Platform ROAS is the same error class: it conditions on who converted, not on cash sales that would have happened without the ad.
If you cannot geo-target it, cannot pause it, or cannot keep control clean, GeoLift is the wrong tool — say the channel is unidentified.
Finance should fund ATT and cash MER together, not a dashboard that moved after the sale already happened.
This SAMPLE uses facebookincubator/GeoLift vignette cities (GeoTestData_PreTest / GeoTestData_Test). No invented DMAs. No client data.
A recovered ATT is still not a license to scale: if the CI misses the effect you needed, you cut or rerun, you do not story-tell.
